# Electric Fish snRNA-seq — NS&B 2026

A hands-on lesson on single-nucleus RNA-seq analysis taught as the genomics
component of the **Electric Fish Module** at the Neural Systems & Behavior
course (Marine Biological Laboratory, Woods Hole), **June 8–13, 2026**.

The biological question: *how does 11-ketotestosterone (11-KT) reshape gene
expression across the electrosensory system at single-cell resolution?*
Students prepare 11-KT/cocoa-butter implants, record EOD waveforms,
flash-freeze tissues, isolate nuclei, and walk through the full
snRNA-seq pipeline — QC, normalization, integration, clustering,
annotation, and differential expression — using real data from
*Brienomyrus brachyistius*.
