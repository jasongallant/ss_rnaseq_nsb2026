---
title: "Electric Fish: Single-Nucleus RNA-seq"
---

This lesson uses **weakly electric mormyrid fish** as a model to teach single-nucleus RNA-seq analysis end to end — from sample preparation and implant surgery through Seurat clustering, pseudobulk differential expression, and cross-study validation.

## Lesson overview

| Episode | Topic | Teaching | Exercises |
|---------|-------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | [Biological Introduction](episodes/introduction.html) | 30 min | 10 min |
| 2 | [11-KT Implantation Protocol](episodes/implant-11kt.html) | 20 min | 75 min |
| 3 | [Examining EOD Duration](episodes/examine-eod-duration.html) | 20 min | 25 min |
| 4 | [snRNA-seq QC](episodes/snrnaseq-qc.html) | 10 min | 2 min |
| 5 | [Normalization & Clustering](episodes/normalize-reduce-cluster.html) | 45 min | 10 min |
| 6 | [Gene Expression Changes](episodes/gene-expression-change.html) | 60 min | 15 min |
