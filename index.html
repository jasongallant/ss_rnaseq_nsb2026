Electric Fish: Single-Nucleus RNA-seq

This lesson uses weakly electric mormyrid fish as a model to teach single-nucleus RNA-seq analysis end to end — from sample preparation and implant surgery through Seurat clustering, pseudobulk differential expression, and cross-study validation.
